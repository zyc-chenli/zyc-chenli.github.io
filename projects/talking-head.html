The problem & my contribution

01

Specify the intent

Developed a text-to-control pipeline that translates language into global affect and local facial-action instructions.

02

Control the motion

Integrated AU-conditioned facial motion with pretrained talking-head generators, supporting region, strength and time-window controls.

03

Measure the response

Built controlled tests across identities to check whether target actions activate and grow with requested strength.

How to interpret these numbers

The controlled study tests 15 AUs on 5 identities, using requested strengths of 0.25, 0.50 and 0.80. A positive response means the strong target action exceeds its baseline; monotonicity checks whether the detector-estimated target intensity increases with the requested strength.

These are local-control diagnostics, not emotion-classification accuracy or a claim of generalisation to all speakers. The system still exhibits cross-region coupling and uneven control for some actions, including blink and lip-related movements. Robust global emotion control remains ongoing work.
